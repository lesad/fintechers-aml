# Further red flags: candidates

Rule C says "include any further red flags you identify". This is the backlog: each candidate with its formula, its basis, what our data says, and what we propose to do with it.

Mentor question 5 is decided by the team (2026-10-19): the CSV columns are fixed, but the `alert_type` values are ours, preferably scoped by the client's AML risk score (low, medium, high). Which candidates ship as their own type is the next call. Until it is made, a further flag raises priority (the "conduct discrepancies" and "multiple flags" factors in [priority.md](priority.md)) and is named in `reason`.

## What the guidance asks for

From the team's pattern research, [aml-pattern-research.md](../challenge/aml-pattern-research.md) (2026-10-19; the primary sources are linked there):

- There is no EU red-flag list yet. AMLA has until 10 July 2027 to publish one (AMLR Art. 69(5)). Until then the usable lists for this data are FAÚ methodological guidance no. 9 on transit accounts (16 September 2024) and the FATF, Interpol and Egmont report on cyber-enabled fraud, Annex A (November 2023).
- Monitoring compares payments with the declared profile (AMLR Art. 26(1), 69(2)) and covers patterns across linked transactions, not only single payments (AMLA draft guidelines on ongoing monitoring, para 44).
- Peer groups may set thresholds, but a normal-looking peer group is no reason to dismiss one client's red flag (AMLA draft paras 54 and 55).
- Every segment threshold needs an internal analysis that justifies it (ČNB benchmark 2/2023). So every threshold below carries the data run that chose it.

## Planted patterns: none found

The data run looked for generator traces in CZK and in raw amounts. None showed ([data-patterns.md](../challenge/data-patterns.md)):

- Amounts just above CZK 100,000: single payments in 2,000 bins from 80,000 to 120,000 decline smoothly (CZK 923 to 560, raw 128 to 39). No band below a threshold is enriched.
- Forwarded shares at 0.80: no bunching (0.75 to 0.80: 57 windows; 0.80 to 0.85: 65).
- Gaps just under 6 h: the time to reach 80% decays over the 6 hours, and the last half hour is the smallest bin (81).
- Same contract date and profile: no identical profiles; at most 3 clients of one counterparty share a contract date; at most 8 clients on one day, a Poisson tail.
- Rule C itself: 1,077 clients forward, 1,080 with the outflow window placed before the inflow.
